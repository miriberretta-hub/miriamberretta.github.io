Infrastructure and Intimate Partner Violence: Evidence from the Tren Maya

with Adán Silveiro Murillo

Using staggered difference-in-differences on hospital injury, crime, and formal employment records across treated municipalities in the Yucatán Peninsula, this paper identifies the effect of large infrastructure construction on intimate partner violence and non-partner female assault.

Floods and Violence Against Women in Mexico

with Daria Denti

A municipality-level difference-in-differences design using SAEH/DGIS hospital records to estimate the effect of flooding events on violence against women, situated within the household-bargaining and economic-shock literature.

War Next Door: The 2022 Invasion and Intimate Partner Violence in Finland

Sole author

A spatial difference-in-differences design using distance to the Russian border as the source of differential exposure, estimating the effect of the invasion of Ukraine on IPV via quarterly, municipality-level Finnish register data.
